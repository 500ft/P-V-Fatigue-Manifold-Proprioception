# P-V RoboSoft v2 — Adaptive Plan

Date: 2026-08-03

The RoboSoft 2027 contributed-paper deadline is 2026-10-15. This plan is separate
from the frozen v1.3 Zenodo/arXiv release.

## Must-have (v2 submission candidate)

- **Citable v1.3 identifier** — publish the exact frozen Zenodo payload if no arXiv
  identifier exists; v2 work must not keep the finished artifact uncitable.
- **Correct statistical unit** — actuator-cluster bootstrap, leave-one-actuator-out
  sensitivity, and independent-unit counts replace the point-level v1 interval in v2.
- **Causal decoupling study** — run coupled positive, false-positive, false-negative,
  leak, recovery, and combined-mechanism interventions from the frozen claim spine.
- **Degradation-family holdout** — reserve at least one entire degradation law for
  final domain-shift evaluation.
- **Matched baselines** — compare loop area, compliance/slope, pressure-decay,
  CUSUM/change point, cycle count, never, and always under the same train-only rule.
- **Probe operating envelope** — quantify noise, quantization, sampling, amplitude,
  frequency, and recovery sensitivity.
- **Task relevance** — report tolerance-normalized error and a tolerance sweep rather
  than treating 0.159 mm as universal.
- **Honest title and architecture** — center the recalibration decision; show normal
  pressure-only estimation separately from the intermittent volumetric P-V probe;
  retain shared-manifold cross-talk as a negative secondary result.
- **Anonymous IEEE manuscript** — six-page core, references included, with author,
  affiliation, repository URLs, and identifying metadata removed for review.
- **Cold review and integrity gate** — independent reviewer checks novelty boundary,
  claims, numbers, anonymity, figures, and reproducibility before submission.

## Nice-to-have (post-core queue)

- **Denser life grid** — improves crossing-time resolution after model-family diversity
  exists; it does not increase the independent sample size.
- **Orientation error** — uses the existing SE(3) output and broadens task relevance at
  moderate implementation cost.
- **Formal health-indicator metrics** — monotonicity, trendability, and prognosability,
  reported with the known same-generator caveat.
- **Three-minute companion video** — concise model, negative result, stress-test map,
  and policy frontier if the paper is otherwise submission-ready.
- **Full reproducibility capsule** — anonymous code/data snapshot if a compliant
  double-blind hosting route is ready.

## Maybe-later (trigger-gated)

- **Physical actuator pilot** · Trigger: a guarded rig, matched actuators, pressure and
  volume acquisition, external pose truth, and enough protected access for repeatable
  baselines are all available with at least six weeks before the internal freeze · Why
  wait: an improvised pilot can add noise, safety risk, and ambiguous evidence.
- **Larger physical validation campaign** · Trigger: the pilot clears its preregistered
  repeatability and directional-consistency gates · Why wait: fabrication and true
  fatigue cycling are high-cost and cannot be rescued by simulation.
- **Multi-material/geometry generalization** · Trigger: at least two fabricated
  actuator families and measurement protocols exist · Why wait: synthetic labels alone
  cannot justify a material-transfer claim.
- **RA-L extension** · Trigger: replicated hardware policy results exist with an
  uncertainty budget and a clear contribution beyond the RoboSoft version · Why wait:
  RA-L evidence expectations are higher than the current simulation package.

## Out

- **Retuning the manifold solely to make the dynamic corrector win** — contradicts the
  frozen negative result and creates post-hoc fishing.
- **More traces from the same generator as the headline upgrade** — adds volume without
  adding mechanism-level evidence.
- **ESN/reservoir-model expansion** — extra tuning and reproducibility cost without
  addressing the main validity problem.
- **Universal fatigue prediction or remaining-useful-life claims** — unsupported by the
  current simulation and proposed pilot.
- **Editing the v1.3 PDF or result spine** — breaks the frozen publication and audit trail.
- **Submitting v1.3 unchanged to RoboSoft** — the statistical unit, novelty focus, and
  causal specificity need v2 treatment first.

## Milestone watch

- **Citable-release gate:** an arXiv or Zenodo identifier resolves publicly and is
  recorded in README, `CITATION.cff`, Progress, and the portfolio.
- **Causal-model gate:** every intervention row is implemented, tested, and produces an
  auditable expected/observed channel map.
- **Domain-shift gate:** one degradation family remains untouched until the final run.
- **Hardware-access gate:** all equipment and protected access conditions above become
  simultaneously true; otherwise the paper remains explicitly simulation-only.
- **Submission gate:** must-haves pass, the paper is anonymous and within length, and a
  cold reviewer finds no claim without committed evidence.
