# Gate 0b: Failure-mode scout, unresolved

The earlier literature-based PASS is withdrawn. A useful P-V warning interval
before failure has not been established for the proposed actuators. The project
remains closed under [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md).

## Evidence and limits

The [Libby full-text check](A01_A04_Literature_Review.md#libby-full-text-check)
records the pressure-angle experiment and its limits. Model drift alone does not
establish when rupture occurs or whether P-V measurements provide useful warning.

[Torzini et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00170-024-14216-0) observed micro-tears
before critical rupture with little effect on actuator performance. Visible damage
can coexist with a weak operational signal. Neither this observation nor the
Libby citation establishes a detectable P-V precursor, its lead time, or its
false-alarm rate.

## Consequence

The former conclusion that a single-specimen spot-check would close this question
is unsupported. Useful warning would require paired signal and failure records,
adequate measurement resolution, and a stated minimum warning time. Those data
are absent here. This correction authorizes no hardware campaign and changes no
stored simulation result.
